# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Participation in this project's issues, pull requests, and other project spaces requires respectful behavior.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, acknowledge other people's work, explain technical disagreements with evidence, respect boundaries, and accept responsibility for mistakes. Give feedback on the work rather than attacking the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information without consent, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. These expectations also apply when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Send conduct reports privately to **ojangoh2@outlook.com**. Include relevant links, a description of the incident, and any immediate concerns. Avoid posting reports containing personal information in public issues.

Dev-Jack is the current maintainer and handles reports on a best-effort basis. Reports will be treated as confidential where possible, although information may need to be shared to investigate or address the incident. There is currently no independent appeal body.

Depending on severity and repetition, maintainers may request a correction, remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban participation. Explain relevant context or request reconsideration through the same private contact. Good-faith reporting and participation in an investigation must not result in retaliation.
